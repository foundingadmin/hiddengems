1. What should the number on a gem mean?

Each tab below is one answer. Click through them and ask one thing of each top 10 list: if every gem with a 90 looked like these, would I trust a 90?

  1. The edge is real and the read is solid. Tab "Edge × clarity". My pick. A 90 means the model and the price disagree a lot, and the evidence behind the model is strong. Odds don't inflate it.
  2. It's likely to hit. Tab "Gate, then safest". A 90 means the likeliest real gem. Best used as the order of the board, not as the number.
  3. It pays the most over time. Tabs "Today's scorecard" and "Expected return". A 90 means a big payout. These fill the top with long shots, so I'd drop them.
