# Time capsules

The reading half of each week's capsule. The raw half (odds, nflverse, forecasts) is saved by the `capsule` job
to the private backup repository (`docs/architecture.md`, "Time capsules").

One folder per week, `2026-wk09/`, holding `news.md`. Saved Saturday night before the board locks, and never
edited after. A backtest reader gets only that week's folder and the raw capsule, with no web access.

Each news item is one line with its player or team, what it says, outlet, URL and capture time (Denver). Cover
every team on the slate: injuries and practice, depth chart and role changes, coaching and play calling, weather,
suspensions, trades and signings, and anything a beat reporter flags about usage.
