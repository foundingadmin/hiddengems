# e1 Gem sheet
- Board rows (table name link, phone row) open a Facet sheet at `?gem=<selection key>`; Back, Escape and Close drop it and focus returns to the row. Full screen under 768px.
- Files: `apps/web/components/gem-sheet.tsx`, `game-log.tsx`, `lib/gem.ts`, `app/page.tsx`, `gems-board.tsx`, `marks.tsx` (Player size and row link, FoolsLabel, no aria-label on spans).
- Core: `packages/core/src/log.ts` `gameLog()` builds Last 10 (TD hit, miss, bye) and the season record, tested in `test/log.test.ts`.
- Facet: `.fc-sheet-section`, `data-phone="full"` and `.fc-game-log` appended to `css/data.css`; docs `components/game-log.md`, `sheet.md`; specimen in `preview/index.html`.
- Data: `lib/gem.ts` reads `player_game_stats`, falling back to `player_usage`, plus best price from `odds_latest`. No schema change, no owner step.
- Skipped per brief: Mined by, price chart, splits by condition, Add to slip. Notes and Context are empty states.
- Sample data has no Fool's Gold; screenshots of one come from a local PGlite pick edited by hand.
