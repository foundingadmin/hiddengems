Say it once

A tab, heading, label, column header, icon or control has already told the reader something. Everything after it builds on that.

Ask before every lineWhat does the reader already know from what sits above and around this?

02

Earn every line

Cut any line whose removal would not reduce understanding. Say it once stops repeats. This stops filler.

Ask before every lineIf this line disappeared, would the reader understand less?

  1. Don'tDo

  2. A lede that describes the layout below it

    Don't

    Each band is a hue's whole contract: its job, its scale and its tokens. Product code uses the semantic token, never the step.

    Do

    Product code uses the semantic token, never the step.

  3. A reason nobody asked for

    Don't
    A chart that plots verdicts borrows the signal colors, since that is their job.
    Do
    A chart that plots verdicts borrows the signal colors.

  4. A line that sells instead of informs

    Don't

    Lessons for every heading, label and sentence. Each one removes a review step, because the page gets it right the first time.

    Do

    Lessons for every heading, label and sentence.

03

Headlines that last

Name the purpose in simple, objective words, and let the content carry the specifics.

Ask before every headlineWill this still be true when the data changes?

  1. Don'tDo

  2. A page title built from this week's numbers

    Don't

    Gesicki carries the widest gap on a seven pick, $50 slate

    Do

    Picks, ranked and explained

  3. A section title that summarizes its contents

    Don't

    Gray by default, and every hue has one job

    Do

    Every hue earns its place

  4. A title split in two with a colon

    Don't

    Color: ten step scales, verbatim

    Do

    Ten steps for every hue

Data-driven statements ("Gesicki carries the widest gap") belong in the content, generated from the data, where they update with it.
04

One word family

Labels that sit together and describe the same thing share one root word, so the reader links them without thinking.

Ask before every labelDoes a word nearby already name this idea?

  1. Don'tDo

  2. A run, its column and its result

    Don't
    ButtonRun now
    ColumnLast run
    DetailTook 4.1s
    Do
    ButtonRun now
    ColumnLast run
    DetailRan 4.1s

  3. A connection and its states

    Don't

    Connect, then Linked, then Unlink

    Do

    Connect, then Connected, then Disconnect

  4. A data action and its record

    Don't

    Refresh odds, last fetched 2m ago, sync failed

    Do

    Pull odds, last pulled 2m ago, pull failed

1 more examples

  1. A bet from button to toast

    Don't

    Track bet, then Wager saved in Logged picks

    Do

    Track bet, then Bet tracked in Tracked bets

Dense screens are read in glances. Every new word is a small question ("is a wager a bet?"), and a screen full of small questions is tiring. One root per idea answers them before they are asked.

House style

Rules that hold on every screen, in every product. The full rules live in foundations/content.md.

Words

Case
Sentence case. Every sentence starts with a capital
Track bet
Titles
No colons in titles, headings or nav labels
Ten steps for every hue
Punctuation
No em dashes or semicolons. Split the sentence
Odds open Tuesday, so the slate is empty
Hedges
Cut "FYI", "Heads up" and "Note"
Inactives post 9:30 AM MT
Plain words
Plain on the fast path, exact in the audit path
Gap, defined where it first appears

Actions

Verbs
One root per idea, everywhere it appears
Run now, Last run, Ran 4.1s
Go-back verbs
Undo your changes, Cancel a flow, Reset to defaults
Undo beside Save
Confirmation
Prefer undo. Confirm only what cannot be taken back
Disconnect FanDuel

Numbers and time

Numbers
Peers share precision. Mixed columns put units in the label
Average gap (pts) +4.1
Times
Clock times in mono, relative times in sans
Today 6:05 PM, 2m ago
Durations
Compact units in values, words in sentences
Ran 4.1s, in 5m
Separators
At most one middle dot per line
TE · Anytime TD scorer

Messages

Notes
One sentence that names the impact
Burke's snap share and role are unchecked
Errors
Say whose problem it is and what happens next
Couldn't pull FanDuel odds
Helpers
A dotted underline always opens a helper
Gap
