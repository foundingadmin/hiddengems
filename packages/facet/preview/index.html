Words that earn their space

Lessons for every heading, label and sentence in the product.

Say it once

A tab, heading, label, column header, icon or control has already told the reader something. Everything after it builds on that instead of saying it again.

One question before every line

What does the reader already know from what sits above and around this? If the new text only restates it, cut it, or rewrite it until it adds something.
