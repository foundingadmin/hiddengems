A tab, heading, label, column header, icon or control has already told the reader something. Everything after it builds on that.

When repeating is right

Confirmations
The button repeats the dialog's verb, so the action is unmistakable."Remove this pick?" then "Remove pick"
Screen readers
An accessible name may restate what sighted readers get from layout.A signal strip reads "5 go, 1 wait"
The record
An audit table may hold values shown earlier, at lower prominence, for exact lookup.The slate summary, then the full ledger

Earn every line

Cut any line whose removal would not reduce understanding. Say it once stops repeats; this stops filler.

Headlines that last

Name the purpose in simple, objective words, and let the content carry the specifics.

Ask before every headlineWill this still be true when the data changes?

  1. Don'tDo

  2. A page title built from this week's numbers

    Don't

    Gesicki carries the widest gap on a seven pick, $50 slate

    Do

    Picks, ranked and explained

  3. A section title that summarizes its contents

    Don't

    Gray by default, and every hue has one job

    Do

    Every hue earns its place

  4. A title split in two with a colon

    Don't

    Color: ten step scales, verbatim

    Do

    Ten steps for every hue

Data-driven statements ("Gesicki carries the widest gap") belong in the content, generated from the data, where they update with it.

House style

Rules that hold on every screen, in every product.

House style rules with an example of each
TopicRuleExample
Words
CaseSentence case for headings, labels and buttonsTrack bet
TitlesNo colons in titles, headings or nav labelsTen steps for every hue
DashesNo em dashes, anywhere. Use a comma, colon, period or parenthesesOdds open Tuesday, so the slate is empty
HedgesCut "FYI", "Heads up" and "Note"Inactives post 9:30 AM MT
VerbsOne verb per action: the button, menu item, toast and confirmation all use itRemove pick, then Pick removed
Plain wordsPlain words on the fast path, exact terms in the audit path; define a term at first useGap, then "our estimate minus the priced chance"
Numbers and time
NumbersPeer values share precision. A lone value keeps its unit beside it; in a column of mixed metrics the word unit moves to the label and changes are percentAverage gap (pts) +4.1, +17%
TimesClock and calendar times in mono, relative times in sans. Relative under 7 days, a date after that, the exact time on hover. Clock times in a column all carry their day word2m ago, Oct 24
HelpersA dotted underline means a helper opens on hover or tap. Use it instead of info icons, and only for thatGap
DurationsCompact units in values, no space, at most two. Words in sentences. A bare duration gets its context wordRan 4.1s, in 5m, 3h ago
SeparatorsAt most one middle dot per metadata line; tables use columnsTE · Anytime TD scorer

NotesOne sentence, active voice, naming the impact. An optional 1 to 2 word label is never repeated by the sentenceBurke's snap share and role are unchecked
