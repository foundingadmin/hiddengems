# MOD3 · app plus read

MOD2 with one variable changed. The read.

## Rules

1. **Candidates.** Every MOD2 gem on the same lock pull, at its MOD2 band and lock price.
2. **Read.** Every candidate gets a read under the [MOD1 read protocol](MOD1.md#read-protocol). Keep, downgrade
   one band (GEM1 downgraded is cut) or cut. A read never raises a band.
3. **Gem.** A candidate still at GEM1 or above after the read. Lock price stays the MOD2 lock price.
4. **Shared reads.** When MOD1 and MOD3 hold the same prop in a segment, one read serves both and is logged once.

## Gem bands

The MOD2 band, less any downgrade from the read.

## Read

The MOD1 read protocol, unchanged, so a MOD3 against MOD2 gap measures the read and nothing else.

## Backtest

The MOD2 screen and bands replay. The read cannot, so a MOD3 back trial equals MOD2's and is not reported on its
own.
