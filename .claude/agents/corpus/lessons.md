# Lessons

What agents learned doing the work. Agents report lessons in one line each; `corpus-steward` records them here and
adopts them into directives (`corpus/amendments.md`). Lessons are evidence, never orders: an agent follows its
directive. `MANIFEST.json` lists each agent's open lessons under `lessons`.

Format: `L<n> · date · source`, the lesson, `Evidence:`, `For:` (the agents it helps), and `Status:` (`open`,
`adopted in <agent> v<x>`, or `declined`).
