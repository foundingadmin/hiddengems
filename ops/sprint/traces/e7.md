# Slips (e7)
- Data: `slips` plus a `slip_legs` table (not jsonb: legs join picks, games and bets by key, a primary key stops duplicates, and History can join grades per leg). Store in `packages/db/src/slips.ts`; migration `0007_slips.sql`; both in `BACKUP_TABLES`. Placing writes one `bets` row per leg; `pull-results` gives parlay legs no payout of their own.
- Core `slips.ts`: `loseItAll` (null plus a correlated flag for same game legs), `slipPayout`, `parseStake`, `buildSlipLink` (DraftKings outcome ids joined with +, DraftKings host only), `slipReminders` (under 2.50, 2+ TD lines; the stake reminder stays off until a usual range is passed in).
- UI: `/slips` This week (`this-week.tsx`), History placeholder (`history-tab.tsx`, for the next builder), handoff at `?view=send` (`send.tsx`), actions in `actions.ts`. For the later entry: `AddToSlip` (`components/add-to-slip.tsx`) and `getCurrentSlip()` (`lib/slips.ts`).
- Facet (`css/slip.css`): `.fc-modal`, editable legs (`.fc-legs[data-editable]`, `.fc-leg-action`), `.fc-link-quiet`; docs in `modal.md`, `cert.md`, `link-action.md`; specimens in `preview/index.html#slips`.
- Sample mode: `build-picks` seeds one draft per week (own play, $15). Suggested play exists only once the Jeweler writes one.
- Owner step: `pnpm db:supabase-sql`, staging first, "Run without RLS". Until then, Slips shows its empty state and actions say the update has not run.
- CI: `jobs.test.ts` drops `slips` and `slip_legs` in the before-migration test; the new slips block runs last.
